Keyboard shortcuts

1Select Deck 1 2Select Deck 2 SpacePlay / pause selected deck CWhile playing, pause and return to the selected deck’s cue point. While paused at a new position, set the cue point. SSync the selected deck to the deck marked MASTER. Load a track into both decks first. ← / →Seek backward / forward five seconds on the selected deck. Focused waveforms use their own seek steps. EnterLoad the selected library track into the selected deck. LMaximize or restore the music library (Esc also restores) Alt + TShow toolbar and focus its pin; Esc dismisses the unpinned toolbar Library dividerDrag or use Up / Down to resize; Home / End for limits; double-click to reset

Mouse & touch controls

Hot Cues 1–4Click an empty pad to set it; click a saved pad to jump and play. Shift-click or right-click deletes it. Large waveformClick to jump or drag horizontally to scrub. Use the zoom controls above it. Small waveformClick or drag to seek across the complete track. When the waveform is focused, Home returns to the start. Elapsed timeClick the restart icon or elapsed-time readout beneath a deck’s small waveform to return to the start. Playback continues if the track is playing. Load a trackUse a track row’s 1 or 2 button to choose a deck, double-click its title to load the selected deck, or drag it onto a jog wheel. Replacing a playing track asks for confirmation. ADD TRACKSClick to choose audio files, or drop files onto the ADD TRACKS button. Jog platterDrag around the platter to scratch / seek. Near the centre spindle, drag left or right. Jog rimDrag the thin outer rim for fine beat nudging. EQ knobsDrag up/down to turn; hold Shift for fine adjustment, double-click to reset, or use arrow keys. Mouse wheelFine seek while hovering over a jog wheel. Hold Shift for smaller steps.

Keyboard shortcuts apply outside text fields, sliders and open dialogs. Use 1 or 2 to select a deck. Hold Shift while dragging a jog wheel for finer movement.

RECOMMENDED SETUP

BrowserGoogle Chrome (recommended), Microsoft Edge
DeviceDesktop or laptop · macOS, Windows or Linux
DisplayOptimised for 1580 × 970 px or larger. Smaller browser windows scroll instead of shrinking the DJ controls.
StorageFree browser storage for your imported tracks. Manage it from LOCAL STORAGE.
Headphone CUETwo separate audio outputs and a compatible browser. Open AUDIO, select a headphone device different from Master, then enable it.
